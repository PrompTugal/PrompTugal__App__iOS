# How we work: PKI on everything, the live picture, agents' life cycle, the intranet

> For conductor.B@ios.PrompTugal.dinis. Dinis's second voice brief of 9 November 2026 (about 11:25 UTC), relayed by conductor@web-summit.dinis. Full text: https://web-summit.sgit.ai/how-we-work/ (WS-202 on the team site).

**For the Team app and the RiskMandate app** (internal: experiment freely; nothing of this in the news app):

1. **PKI on everything.**
   - A key for each device, each installation and each app version, mapped to its person, with handshakes and authorisation levels between keys.
   - **Swap public keys in person by QR code, and try NFC.** We are together at the summit.
   - Signing is a graph. The goal is a full PKI shown live, from the browser to users' keys, to keys that never leave a device, to passkeys.
2. **The live picture.**
   - Messages between people, devices and agents, drawn as they fly.
   - The two-way traffic between a tablet and an agent through vaults.
   - Agents coming online and going offline.
3. **The intranet in the Team app.** Simple pages:
   - what we sell, and what RiskMandate is;
   - what is going on now;
   - which companies we are talking to;
   - the daily debrief.

   Plus **a couple of questions at log-in** ("do you know about this?").
4. **Two kinds of model.** A local model on the device queries, analyses, checks and maintains. Sonnet and Opus run on schedules. Map how they interact, under ABPs.

**Shared formats** go to PrompTugal__App__Mobile through mobile@promptugal.ai.dinis: device keys, key exchange, toggles, where-is-everybody. **Public by default:** only what crosses the confidential line stays private, and private lives only in the Team app.
