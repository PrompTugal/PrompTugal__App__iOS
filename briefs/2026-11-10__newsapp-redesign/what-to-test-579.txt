PrompTugal: Lisbon Summit, the independent companion app for the Lisbon tech summit, 9 to 12 November. No account needed.

New in this build: watch the interviews.
- News has a new Videos tab with our editors' interviews from the summit. Tap one to watch it in the app, full screen if you like.
- A video takes a moment to load the first time (about 26 MB for three minutes). After that it plays again without a connection.
- Still new from the last builds: a lighter look with a greeting by the time in Lisbon, five tabs, Show on the map on every talk, one tap to pick a talk and add it to your calendar, and where to meet RiskMandate in More.

Please try:
- Open News, then Videos, and watch an interview. Try it once on Wi-Fi and again in airplane mode.
- Star a talk, find it on the map, and check it is in your calendar.

Tell us with Send Beta Feedback (take a screenshot in the app, or open TestFlight): what was confusing, what was missing, and anything that broke. Please include what you were trying to do.
