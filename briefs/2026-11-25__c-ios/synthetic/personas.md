# Synthetic users for PrompTugal.news (c.ios.synthetic, 25 Nov 2026)

> Eight fictional readers. None is a real person; any likeness is chance. Each card: who, device, what they want, what would make them delete the app. The walk-throughs are in [walkthroughs.md](walkthroughs.md).

## P1 · Graça, Loulé resident

- **Who:** 58, runs a small shop off Loulé's market, follows the council's notices and the weather for her stall.
- **Device:** iPhone SE (2nd gen, 4.7"), iOS 18, often one bar of signal or none inside the market hall. Default text size.
- **Wants:** what is happening in Loulé this week, council news, rain tomorrow, in two taps.
- **Deletes it if:** the first screen is a spinner on poor signal, the news is about AI rather than Loulé, or it shows yesterday's weather as today.

## P2 · Rui, Lisbon tech journalist

- **Who:** 34, covers startups and AI policy for a national outlet. Checks every claim against the original.
- **Device:** iPhone 16 Pro, iOS 26, good signal, Apple Intelligence on.
- **Wants:** to see where each fact comes from, who wrote it (person or agent), and when it was read.
- **Deletes it if:** a story has no source link, a date is wrong, or the app says "independent" while its own disclosure says otherwise.

## P3 · Marta, Algarve council officer

- **Who:** 45, communications officer at one of the 16 Algarve councils.
- **Device:** iPhone 15 (work phone), iOS 26, MDM-managed, location off by policy.
- **Wants:** to know what the app says about her council, whether it is accurate, and how to get a correction.
- **Deletes it if:** her council is described wrongly with no way to correct it, or the app shows notices that are days old as current.

## P4 · Tiago, Faro startup founder

- **Who:** 31, co-founder of a five-person software company in Faro.
- **Device:** iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 26.
- **Wants:** to see if his company is on Faro's "AI map", who else is, events, investors and programmes nearby.
- **Deletes it if:** the map misses him with no way to add himself, or the "Work with us" path leads nowhere.

## P5 · Hannah, tourist in Lagos

- **Who:** 29, from Bristol, a week in Lagos in late November. Speaks no Portuguese.
- **Device:** iPhone 14, iOS 26, roaming data, location allowed.
- **Wants:** what is on in Lagos tonight and this weekend, the weather and the sea, things near her.
- **Deletes it if:** "near me" is hard to find, events are past, or it feels like a tech-industry app, not a town guide.

## P6 · Sr. Joaquim, Portuguese-speaking retiree

- **Who:** 74, retired teacher in Tavira. Reads Portuguese only.
- **Device:** iPhone 13, iOS 26, Dynamic Type at Accessibility XL, VoiceOver on for long reads.
- **Wants:** news of Tavira in Portuguese, read aloud.
- **Deletes it if:** half the app stays in English after he picks Português, or VoiceOver reads him buttons with no names.

## P7 · App Store reviewer

- **Who:** App Review, testing the next submission of PrompTugal.news against the listing and the review notes.
- **Device:** iPad Pro 13", iPadOS 26, fresh install, **location permission denied**, no Apple Intelligence model downloaded, landscape.
- **Wants:** every feature in the description works; nothing is placeholder, beta, or a purchase path without In-App Purchase; privacy policy matches behaviour.
- **Rejects it if:** see [app-review-risk.md](app-review-risk.md).

## P8 · Sofia, investor

- **Who:** 50, partner at a seed fund, heard about "the agents newsroom" and wants to see it working.
- **Device:** iPad Air, iPadOS 26, and an iPhone 16.
- **Wants:** evidence the agents run on their own, what people decide, what it costs, how sourcing is enforced.
- **Deletes it if:** the "live" board is a replay without saying so, numbers are simulated without a badge, or the app looks like a leftover conference app.
