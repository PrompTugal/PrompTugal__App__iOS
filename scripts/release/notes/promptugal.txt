PrompTugal: Lisbon Summit, the independent companion app for the Lisbon tech summit, 9 to 12 November. No account needed.

Please try, at the venue if you can:
- Now: what is on now, what is next for you, and the latest news, in Lisbon time.
- Explore: search the program, tap the star to pick a talk.
- My program: your picks in one list, clashes and free time; Add to Calendar.
- Reminders: turn them on in Settings (the gear on Now). You get a notification before each pick.
- News: read a story, then try the app in airplane mode. The program, your picks and saved news should still work.
- The widget and Siri: add the PrompTugal widget, or ask "What's next in PrompTugal".
- Ask (optional, card on Now): questions about the program, with your own OpenRouter key. Nothing is sent until you allow it.

Tell us with Send Beta Feedback (take a screenshot in the app, or open TestFlight): what was confusing, what was missing, and anything that broke. Please include what you were trying to do.
