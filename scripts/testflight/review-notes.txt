PrompTugal is an independent companion app for participants of Web Summit Lisbon (9 to 12 November 2026), made by PrompTugal.news, a newsroom covering AI in Portugal. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Web Summit; the app says so in Settings > About, and the store name, icon and screenshots do not use the event's name or marks.

NO ACCOUNT IS NEEDED. There is no sign-up and no login. Every main screen works as soon as the app opens.

THINGS TO TRY
1. Now (first tab). The date and time in Lisbon, what is on now and next, and the latest news. On iPhone, the gear at the top opens Settings.
2. Explore (third tab). The full summit program in Lisbon time, built into the app and refreshed from promptugal.news. Search "AI" and tap the star on a session to pick it. Tapping a session opens its details.
3. My program (second tab). Your picks in one list, with clashes and free time, and Add to Calendar (write-only calendar access; the app cannot read the calendar).
4. Reminders. The notification permission is asked the first time you pick a talk, or when you turn reminders on in Settings > Reminders. A local notification is scheduled before each pick.
5. News (fourth tab). Stories and newsletter editions from PrompTugal.news. Turn on airplane mode: the program, your picks and cached news still work.
6. Widget and Siri. Add the PrompTugal widget for what is next, or ask Siri "What's next in PrompTugal".

WHAT THE APP SENDS
- Public feeds are read from promptugal.news; nothing about the user is sent with them.
- Picks, plans and reminders stay on the device.
- The app talks to one host only: promptugal.news (public content). It collects no data: no cookies, no telemetry, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDK.
Privacy policy: https://ios.app.promptugal.news/privacy.html (also Settings > About > Privacy). Support: https://ios.app.promptugal.news/support.html (contact address on that page, answered within 48 hours).

AGENDA SOURCE. The program is the summit's public schedule, republished on promptugal.news by our newsroom; each session opens its page there. We are an independent news site covering the event.

NO USER-GENERATED CONTENT. No posts, comments or messages between people.

NO PURCHASES. No in-app purchases or subscriptions; payment pages are not opened inside the app.

ENCRYPTION. Only Apple's encryption (CryptoKit, Keychain, HTTPS); ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption is NO.
