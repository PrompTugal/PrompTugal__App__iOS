PrompTugal: Lisbon Summit, the independent companion app for the Lisbon tech summit, 9 to 12 November. No account needed.

New in this build: a simpler layout.
- Five tabs: Now, My program, Explore, Map and News. The venue map is now one tap away.
- The round More button at the top right of every tab has reminders, help, privacy and support.
- Tap a talk on Now to open it in the app, with the star and Add to Calendar.
- Every talk has Show on the map.

Please try, at the venue if you can:
- Find a talk in Explore, tap the star, then find it on the map.
- Turn on reminders in More. You get a notification before each pick.
- Read a story in News, then try the app in airplane mode. The program, your picks and saved news should still work.

Tell us with Send Beta Feedback (take a screenshot in the app, or open TestFlight): what was confusing, what was missing, and anything that broke. Please include what you were trying to do.
