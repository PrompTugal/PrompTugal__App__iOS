## The catch: the shell is not the root view yet

`PrompTugalApp.swift` still opens `WebsiteView()` at launch. `AppShell` exists and builds, but it is used only in previews, and `.teamFeatures()` (which starts the bridge, reminders and the meter) is not applied. So on a phone built from `dev` right now:

- the new dashboards, news, graph, vault and debug screens are compiled in but cannot be opened;
- the website does not yet talk to the app through the bridge, and no reminders are scheduled.

**The site now checks this.** A screen counts toward a feature only when `AppShell` is the root view of the app's `WindowGroup`, and the bridge and reminders count only once `.teamFeatures()` is applied there. For a few minutes after the merge the product pages showed 14 of 19 features as in the beta; they now show 2 (the website in the app, and the offline reader) until the root view moves. The [screens page](../screens.html) says so at the top.
