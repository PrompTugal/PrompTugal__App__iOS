## Screenshots

- The first successful `screens.yml` runs on dev (runs 4 and 5) produced 20 images each, for an iPhone and an iPad simulator.
- **Two problems in those runs.** The export cut screen ids at the first dot, so `news.home`, `news.newsroom` and `news.graph` all became `news` and overwrote each other. And the iPad images were rotated. Platform fixed both at the source (exact ids, a check for exactly 13 images per device, iPad upright); it reached dev at 06:14 (`745fd75`).
- **What the site does meanwhile.** It publishes only images whose name is a screen the app registers, and only iPhone images until an iPad image from a fixed run has been looked at. The live site shows **5 of 29 screens** (My day, Briefed, Debug, Settings, Vault). The next run fills in the rest.

## What the screenshots found

Design looked at the first images and fixed, at 05:57 (`9582dd9`):

- "in in 1 day" on the stand-up countdown;
- "updated" times in the future;
- the web-original page on iPhone, whose title leaked into the navigation bar (it is now a sheet);
- iPhone tab titles too long for the tab bar.

At 05:58 every opened screen got an accessibility identifier (`pt.destination.<id>`), so the screenshot test can check it landed on the right screen. At 05:41, Ask became available beside any screen on iPad.
