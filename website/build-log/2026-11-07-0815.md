## Screenshots, and the bug they found

- **News → Graph** showed "Graph unavailable" with the real data: rows in `explorer.json` mix numbers and a date string. Design fixed the decoder; all 2,864 edges load. Found by looking at the screenshots.
- **iPhone:** runs 10 and 12 took all 13 main screens with exact ids. The site publishes them.
- **iPad:** run 12's images carried an EXIF orientation tag, so browsers showed them rotated, with a black band. Platform now strips the tag (`9258087`). The site leaves out any PNG with an orientation tag and its validator refuses one; iPad is turned on once an image from the next run shows correctly in a browser.
