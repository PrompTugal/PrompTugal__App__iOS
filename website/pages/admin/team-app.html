The Team app: what your people and agents are doing

The Team app is the PrompTugal team’s own app, for Web Summit Lisbon and after. It is not in the App Store and not for the public: the team installs it from its own TestFlight. On iPad it shows who is doing what, what is waiting on whom and what the agents reported, and it keeps private work in encrypted vaults. How it works

Public data, private vaults

  • What the team publishes, the app reads. The dashboards are built from JSON the team’s site already publishes: the people, each person’s tasks and decisions, the stand-up briefings, and the agents’ wakes, reports, pulse and log. The app caches it, so it works offline.
  • What is private stays encrypted. Notes, messages and shared picks go into vaults. Each file is encrypted on the device before it leaves; the server holds ciphertext. Only people with the vault’s keys can read it.
  • iPad first. The iPad has room for a sidebar and a dashboard side by side. What works well there goes back to the website.
  • Everything is visible, when you want it. Debug mode, turned on in Settings, shows every request the app makes, its size and timing, every vault read and decryption step, and which keys exist on the device, never their values.

What makes it possible

100+ agents, each with a behaviour policy

Our team works with more than 100 agents, and they can work together because each one has an Agent Behaviour Policy (ABP), from RiskMandate. An ABP is a written description, for one agent in one deployment, of what it can really reach, what it was asked to do, the gap between the two, and what actually stands in the way.

  • Every agent has one. The team publishes an ABP for each of its agents, in RiskMandate’s terms: Reach, Mandate, Gap, Barriers and Provenance.
  • Observe first, then lock down. The policies are in an explorer phase: they write down each gap with its evidence and a proposal, and block only what is really dangerous. Stricter, deny-by-default policies come next, one step at a time, each with a way for a person to still do the job by hand.
  • The app shows the result. The Agents, Wall and Briefed screens show what each agent did, how its wakes went, and what it is asking a person for.
