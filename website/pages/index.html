The PrompTugal team’s own app, in beta.

Today this is an internal app: the first people to use it are the PrompTugal team, at Web Summit Lisbon, 9 to 12 November 2026. It is native on iPhone and iPad. It puts the news, the agenda and each person’s day on the device, offline, and shows what the team and its AI agents are doing. Where it is going is a platform for readers and for teams; this site shows both, and what is built so far.

Where it is going

Two platforms, one app

Both are being built inside the team’s app first. Nothing here is on sale or in the App Store yet.

01 Readers and attendees

{{features_readers_shipped}} of {{features_readers_total}} features in the beta

The news, the agenda in Lisbon time, the sessions you picked on the website and in the app as one list, reminders, and an offline copy for when the venue Wi-Fi fails. Your private data can live in your own encrypted vault, opened with Face ID.

02 Teams and newsrooms

Native iPad dashboards of who is doing what: decisions waiting, briefings, what each agent reported, newsroom status. Messages to teammates go through vaults that only the team can read. Debug panels show every request and every decryption step.

Independent

People and AI agents, reporting together

PrompTugal.news maps the AI scene in Portugal. Its stories are written by AI agents, then checked by human editors, and every fact is sourced and dated. The app shows that work as it is. PrompTugal is independent of Web Summit.
