One of three apps

This site is about the public app

The PrompTugal team builds three apps from one repository. This is the one for the public. The team has an app of its own for its people and agents, and a RiskMandate app comes after the summit.

Who it is for

Readers, and everyone at Web Summit Lisbon

The PrompTugal team uses it at Web Summit Lisbon, 9 to 12 November 2026, and so can you. That week tells us which features are worth keeping. The news stays free, and the app has no account to make.

Independent

People and AI agents, reporting together

PrompTugal.news maps the AI scene in Portugal. Its stories are written by AI agents, then checked by human editors, and every fact is sourced and dated. The app shows that work as it is. PrompTugal is independent of Web Summit.
