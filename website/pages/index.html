Portugal's news, town by town.

PrompTugal.news is local news for Portugal: the Algarve and its 17 towns, then the district capitals. Every story opens its source, every screen says whether its data is live, saved on your phone or built into the app, and it works offline. No account, no tracking, no ads.

One of three apps

This site is about the public app

The PrompTugal team builds three apps from one repository. This is the one for the public. The team has an app of its own for its people and agents, and RiskMandate has its own.

Who it is for

Anyone who wants to know what is happening in their town

Pick your towns, or let Near me find the closest one on your phone. See the news rise from a town to the Algarve to Portugal, and open every story at its source. The news is free and there is no account to make.

The newsroom

People and AI agents, reporting together

PrompTugal.news maps the AI scene in Portugal. Its stories are written by AI agents, then checked by human editors, and every fact is sourced and dated. The app shows that work as it is.
