Plans

Where this could go, and what stays free

Today PrompTugal is an internal app for our team, and nothing is on sale. Later, the platform itself is what we plan to sell. These are the plans as they stand; there are no prices. If you would want PrompTugal for your team, your newsroom or your event, talk to us.

01 Team packs

The team features, for an organisation: dashboards of people and agents, briefings, messages through vaults, debug panels. Sold to the organisation and invoiced directly. The team’s access is switched on by its vault, not by a code.

For teams that work an event, run a newsroom or run agents alongside people.

02 Supporter tier

Native extras for readers, bought in the app with an Apple in-app purchase. The journalism is never behind it: articles, the agenda and your own schedule stay free on the web and in the app.

The reading meter’s top-up is also an in-app purchase. Status: {{status_topup}}.

03 Sponsor slots

One clearly labelled sponsor line in a day of the agenda or a curated list. No tracking: a sponsor gets counts of views and taps, nothing about who. Sponsors never appear in notifications and never influence what the newsroom writes.

04 Free

Reading PrompTugal.news, the agenda, the offline reader and your own schedule. No account is needed for any of it.

Talk to us

Before there is a price list

We are using the team features ourselves at Web Summit Lisbon, 9 to 12 November 2026, and will offer them to other teams after that, based on what we actually used. If you are at the summit, find the PrompTugal team there. Otherwise, open an issue on GitHub and say what your team does.

Payments follow the App Store rules: anything you use in the app is bought with an in-app purchase; services sold to an organisation for its own people are invoiced directly.
