Plans

Where this could go, and what stays free

Today PrompTugal is in beta, free, and nothing is on sale. Later, the platform itself is what we plan to sell. These are the plans as they stand; there are no prices. If you would want PrompTugal for your team, your newsroom or your event, talk to us.

01 For other teams

The team’s own app has dashboards of people and agents, briefings, messages through vaults and debug panels. It is internal today and not in the App Store. After the summit we may offer the same to other teams. That would be a separate app and an offer to the organisation, invoiced directly, not part of the public app.

For teams that work an event, run a newsroom or run agents alongside people. Which app is which.

02 Supporter tier

Native extras for readers, bought in the app with an Apple in-app purchase. The journalism is never behind it: articles, the agenda and your own schedule stay free on the web and in the app.

The reading meter’s top-up would also be an in-app purchase. It is hidden in release builds until credit does something in the app. Status in the PrompTugal app: {{status_topup}}.

03 Sponsor slots

One clearly labelled sponsor line in a day of the agenda or a curated list. No tracking: a sponsor gets counts of views and taps, nothing about who. Sponsors never appear in notifications and never influence what the newsroom writes.

04 Free

Reading PrompTugal.news, the agenda, the offline reader and your own schedule. No account is needed for any of it.

Talk to us

Before there is a price list

We are using the team’s own app ourselves at Web Summit Lisbon, 9 to 12 November 2026, and may offer it to other teams after that, based on what we actually used. If you are at the summit, find the PrompTugal team there. Otherwise, open an issue on GitHub and say what your team does.

Payments follow the App Store rules: anything you use in the app is bought with an in-app purchase; services sold to an organisation for its own people are invoiced directly.
