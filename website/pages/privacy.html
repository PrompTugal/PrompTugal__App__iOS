Privacy

What the app keeps, and where

PrompTugal {{version}} has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. It collects nothing about you.

What the app requests

  • The offline reader downloads three public feeds from promptugal.news: the live agenda, individual event records and the news RSS feed. They are the same files anyone can open in a browser.
  • The website tabs load the websites in your sidebar, like any browser would. Each website’s own privacy policy covers what it does.
  • Links that leave a website open in Safari, not in the app.

What stays on your device

  • Saved sessions and cached feeds. Stored in the app’s private storage so the reader works offline. They are never uploaded.
  • Website data. Cookies and local storage used by the websites you open, such as your theme and schedule picks on PrompTugal.news. This is kept apart from Safari.
  • Your website list. The Websites.json file you can see and edit in the Files app.

Deleting the app removes all of it.

TestFlight

While PrompTugal is in beta, Apple’s TestFlight shares your email address, device model, OS version and any feedback or crash reports you send with the team. Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice has the details.

Checking this

The whole app is open source. The network requests are in Content.swift and the website tabs are in WebsiteView.swift, both on GitHub.
