At the summit

Built for walking between halls

  • Lisbon time, always. Every session is shown in Lisbon time, whatever time zone your phone is in. A session that ends after midnight ends on the next day.
  • Offline first. Open the app once while you are online. After that, the agenda, your saved sessions and the latest stories are on the device. If a refresh fails, what you had stays on screen with the time of the last good sync.
  • One schedule. PrompTugal.news already lets you add sessions to your day in the browser. The app reads and writes the same list, in the same format (nr.schedule.v1), so you do not keep two.
  • The website is still the website. Articles, company and person pages, search and the graph stay on PrompTugal.news and open inside the app. The app adds what a browser cannot do: reminders, Face ID, a private store and offline lists.

Private by design

Your data in your own vault

Your picks and notes can be kept in an encrypted vault that you hold the keys to. The app reads it with Apple’s own cryptography (AES-256-GCM, HMAC and HKDF in CryptoKit). The server it syncs with stores only ciphertext and opaque file names. The key stays on your device, in the Keychain, behind Face ID.
